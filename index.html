5 nights from Canberra to the mountains

24–29 December 2026 · 6 days / 5 nights · Sydney return

A Christmas road trip with a one-year-old: Canberra city views, alpine walks and snow-gum scenery. Comfortable stays with private bathrooms, time for naps, and space to change the plan.

Fri 25 Dec · Day 2

Christmas at the Arboretum and the lake

Typical weather · not a forecastCanberra: average high ~27°C / low ~12°C · cool early and late
Canberra Airport ↗
Walking and paceShort walks, picnic and a nap break
OvernightCanberra · 2 of 2 nights

Local driving · allow 45–75 min across the day

  • Morning: Explore the National Arboretum grounds and views around Dairy Farmers Hill. Take a look at the POD playground; its higher climbing pods are mainly for older children.
  • Midday: Return for lunch and your baby’s nap. Bring Christmas food rather than depending on cafés.

Sat 26 Dec · Day 3

Mount Ainslie, then south to Jindabyne

Typical weather · not a forecastCanberra ~27°C / 12°C → Jindabyne region ~24°C / 9°C · average high / low
Canberra Airport ↗ · Cooma Airport regional reference ↗
Walking and paceDrive-up summit · short wander + travel
OvernightJindabyne · 1 of 3 nights

Canberra → Cooma → Jindabyne · about 2½ hr intercity driving, plus local lookout access and breaks

  • Morning: Have breakfast, then drive to Mount Ainslie summit. Allow 45–60 minutes for the Canberra panorama, photographs and a short wander. This is the main morning stop, not an optional extra.
  • Late morning and lunch: Leave Canberra and head towards Cooma. Stop for lunch, feeding and time out of the car at Centennial Park, with a playground, picnic areas, toilets and baby-change facilities. Allow roughly 45–60 minutes, or longer if needed.
  • Afternoon: Continue to Jindabyne, check into an ensuite cabin or apartment, stock the kitchen and have a relaxed evening.

Driving to Mount Ainslie summit gives you the views without making the steep summit hike part of a transfer day. Cooma is a practical lunch and park break; no Discovery Centre visit or other ticketed attraction is scheduled. Arrive in Jindabyne during the afternoon without rushing the lookout.

Sun 27 Dec · Day 4

Thredbo and the Kosciuszko lookout

Typical weather · not a forecastLift-top reference: average high ~14°C / low ~5°C · wind, rain and sudden cold possible
Thredbo Top Station reference ↗
Walking and pace4km return · exposed alpine walking
OvernightJindabyne · 2 of 3 nights

About 1–1½ hr driving return · allow 4–6 hr for the full outing

  • Morning: Drive to Thredbo and take the scenic lift, subject to operations and confirmed infant/carrier arrangements.
  • The walk: Follow the Kosciuszko lookout route: 4km return, about 1–2 hours walking before baby breaks. Enjoy granite formations and open alpine scenery.

Confirm how your baby and backpack carrier can travel on the lift before buying tickets. Choose the calmest of the two mountain days for this walk. A village visit or Lake Jindabyne rest day is the lower-effort alternative.

Mon 28 Dec · Day 5

Charlotte Pass and the Snow Gums boardwalk

Typical weather · not a forecastAlpine reference ~14°C / 5°C; lower regional reference ~24°C / 9°C · average high / low; local conditions vary
Thredbo Top Station reference ↗ · Cooma Airport regional reference ↗
Walking and pace400m return boardwalk · steps and exposure
OvernightJindabyne · 3 of 3 nights

About 1½–2 hr driving return from Jindabyne · allow 3–4 hr overall for the outing

  • Morning: Drive to Charlotte Pass for the Snow Gums boardwalk, twisted alpine trees and views towards the Main Range. Allow 15–30 minutes walking before photographs and baby breaks; the boardwalk includes steps and exposed viewpoints.
  • Lunch: Picnic at Sawpit Creek on the way back down. Keep it a simple picnic stop rather than adding another hike.

This is deliberately the shorter outing after Thredbo. Short distance still needs warm layers and suitable conditions. Swap 27 and 28 December if that gives the exposed lift-top walk a calmer forecast; both nights are in the same base.

3. Other choices, without overfilling the day

Choose one Canberra neighbourhood for the arrival evening. Keep Mount Ainslie on the departure morning and the two mountain days clear for the main outings.

A 2–3-hour city wander and early meal. Braddon suits cafés and small shops; NewActon adds Nishi’s architecture. Choose a precinct on the arrival evening rather than driving between them all.

More demanding hikes and other stops we discussed

Mount Kosciuszko summit: about 13km return from the lift top, 4–5 hours before breaks. Dead Horse Gap: 5km one-way, Grade 4, 1½–2½ hours; arrange pickup or add the return river walk. Small walks, big weather

Summer alpine weather can change quickly. Bring warm, waterproof and sun-protective layers for both you and the baby. Short distance does not imply pram access: steps, wet rock, creek crossings and wind matter. Keep a turnaround option and choose dry, stable footing.

Alpine safety ↗ Live Traffic NSW ↗

Five nights, two bases

24–29 December is six calendar days and five nights: two in Canberra and three in Jindabyne. Mount Ainslie is a firm stop on 26 December; Thredbo and Charlotte Pass have separate days. The final day is for driving and substantial breaks. A sixth night would split that drive rather than add sightseeing.
