7 nights from Canberra to the high country

Thu 24 Dec · Day 1

Sydney to Canberra, via Goulburn

Typical weather · not a forecastCanberra: average high ~27°C / low ~12°C · cool early and late
Canberra Airport ↗
Walking and paceEasy stops + optional summit walk
OvernightCanberra · 1 of 2 nights

About 3½–4 hr driving, before lunch and baby breaks

  • Morning: Leave Sydney and break in Goulburn. Belmore Park has picnic tables, a playground and toilets; the Big Merino is an optional separate photo stop.
  • Afternoon: Check in, buy Christmas groceries and settle in. Choose Civic and Braddon for a short wander and early dinner.
  • Optional lookout: Drive to Mount Ainslie summit for a city panorama if there is energy left; otherwise save it for tomorrow. Allow 30–45 minutes at the lookout.

Mount Ainslie is a priority stop. Driving to the summit keeps this arrival day relaxed. The optional Kokoda summit walk is 4.5km return with 250m ascent, steep sections and steps; allow roughly 1½–2 hours plus breaks if choosing the walk instead.

A different city evening

Choose NewActon’s Nishi architecture or Kingston Foreshore’s waterfront promenade and dinner instead of Braddon. Pick one precinct; restaurant Christmas Eve hours need checking.

Fri 25 Dec · Day 2

Christmas at the Arboretum and the lake

Typical weather · not a forecastCanberra: average high ~27°C / low ~12°C · bring a layer for the sunrise start
Canberra Airport ↗
Walking and paceShort walks, picnic and a nap break
OvernightCanberra · 2 of 2 nights

Local driving · allow 45–75 min across the day

  • Morning: Explore the National Arboretum grounds and views around Dairy Farmers Hill. Take a look at the POD playground; its higher climbing pods are mainly for older children.
  • Midday: Return for lunch and your baby’s nap. Bring Christmas food rather than depending on cafés.
  • Afternoon: Take a short Lake Burley Griffin stroll around Commonwealth Park or the National Carillon. Add Mount Ainslie’s drive-up lookout here if you skipped it yesterday.

Arboretum grounds and the POD playground are listed as open daily. The visitor centre, café and bonsai collection close on 25 December. Keep this a flexible outdoor Christmas, especially if the day is hot.

Optional sunrise balloon flight

If Balloon Aloft Canberra confirms a 25 December service, eligible adults can fly before a later, shorter Arboretum visit. Meet about 30 minutes before sunrise at the Hyatt Hotel Canberra; allow 3–4 hours total for a 45–60-minute flight, plus an extra hour if choosing breakfast. Minimum age is 6: your one-year-old cannot fly, so arrange a non-flying adult to care for them. Christmas availability and the exact meeting time are unconfirmed; flights depend on weather. Keep the original morning plan if it does not run.

Sat 26 Dec · Day 3

Questacon, Mini Q and the journey south

Typical weather · not a forecastCanberra ~27°C / 12°C → Jindabyne region ~24°C / 9°C · average high / low
Canberra Airport ↗ · Cooma Airport proxy ↗
Walking and paceIndoor play + travel day
OvernightJindabyne · 1 of 4 nights

Canberra → Cooma → Jindabyne · about 2½ hr driving

  • 9am–noon: Visit Questacon, including Mini Q’s play space for ages 0–6. Book general admission ahead. Mini Q may use timed sessions allocated on arrival in busy periods.
  • Lunch and drive: Have lunch, then head south. Pause in Cooma for feeding and a proper break before continuing to Jindabyne.
  • Late afternoon: Check into an ensuite cabin or apartment, stock the kitchen and have a quiet evening.

Prams park outside Mini Q; it has baby-change and feeding facilities. Snowy Hydro Discovery Centre in Cooma publishes Boxing Day hours of 9am–2pm, so save the exhibits for the return journey if leaving Canberra after lunch.

Animals or bushland instead

Replace Questacon with the National Zoo & Aquarium, allowing about 3 hours from its 9:30am opening, OR The Sanctuary at Tidbinbilla. Tidbinbilla adds access driving: allow roughly 3½–4½ hours total driving for the day plus a 2-hour visit. These replace Questacon, not additions.

Sun 27 Dec · Day 4

Charlotte Pass and the Snow Gums boardwalk

Typical weather · not a forecastAlpine reference: average high ~14°C / low ~5°C · exposed lookout; cold wind and rain possible
Thredbo Top Station reference ↗
Walking and pace400m return boardwalk · steps and exposure
OvernightJindabyne · 2 of 4 nights

About 1½–2 hr driving return from Jindabyne

  • Morning: Drive up to Charlotte Pass. Follow the Snow Gums boardwalk for twisted alpine trees and Main Range views; allow 15–30 minutes walking before photo and baby breaks.
  • Lunch: Stop at Sawpit Creek picnic area on the way back down.
  • Optional extension: Walk Sawpit’s 2km Grade 3 loop if everyone is comfortable. Allow another 45–75 minutes walking, plus breaks; otherwise just picnic.

A short boardwalk still needs warm layers and calm conditions. There are steps at Snow Gums, while Sawpit is an uneven carrier walk rather than a paved pram stroll. Keep the afternoon free if the morning is enough.

Thredbo and the Kosciuszko lookout

Thredbo Top Station reference ↗
Walking and pace4km return · exposed alpine walking
OvernightJindabyne · 3 of 4 nights

About 1–1½ hr driving return · allow 4–6 hr for the full outing

  • Morning: Drive to Thredbo and take the scenic lift, subject to operations and confirmed infant/carrier arrangements.

Tue 29 Dec · Day 6

Rainbow Lake or Porcupine Rocks

Typical weather · not a forecastHigh-country reference ~14°C / 5°C; Jindabyne region ~24°C / 9°C · average high / low; lower walks can be warmer
Thredbo Top Station reference ↗ · Cooma Airport proxy ↗
Walking and paceChoose one main walk; both only as a stretch
OvernightJindabyne · 4 of 4 nights

Roughly 1–1½ hr driving return for one outing; allow 1½–2 hr if combining

  • Easier main choice: Walk Rainbow Lake: 2.5km return, 45–75 minutes published walking time. Add a picnic and allow around 2–3 hours overall.
  • Longer main choice: Choose Porcupine Rocks instead: 5.7km return, 1½–2½ hours walking before baby breaks. Snow gums, alpine meadows and granite outcrops lead to valley views.
  • Rest choice: A Lake Jindabyne stroll, café and accommodation break are a worthwhile alternative if you want to slow down.

Both walks are priorities to consider, not an obligation to do both today. Combining them means about 8.2km and 2¼–3¾ hours published walking time, before feeds, rests and transfers: a substantial carrier day. Only do both if you already manage that load comfortably and conditions suit. Otherwise choose one or add a Jindabyne night. Avoid scrambling onto Porcupine’s rocks; Rainbow has small creek crossings.

Thu 31 Dec · Day 8

Fitzroy Falls, then home to Sydney

Typical weather · not a forecastHighlands reference: average high ~24°C / low ~12°C · pack a rain layer for the falls
Moss Vale reference ↗
Walking and pace150m each way to the first lookout · step-free
OvernightHome · no overnight

About 2–3 hr driving total for a simple falls-to-home route

  • Morning: Visit Fitzroy Falls. The main viewpoint is 150m from the visitor centre along a step-free walkway; allow 30–60 minutes for an unhurried stop.
  • Optional walk: Continue along part of the West Rim track for more gorge views if time and footing suit.
  • Lunch and home: Have lunch in a Highlands village and head to Sydney, allowing for New Year’s Eve traffic.

Fitzroy is the main recommendation. Carrington Falls plus Nellies Glen is a worthwhile alternative if you want a dedicated waterfall half-day, but is not essential after Fitzroy. Carrington’s 600m loop includes steep metal stairs; choose the outing rather than adding every waterfall.

Thu 31 Dec · Day 8

Yarrangobilly caves and thermal pool

Typical weather · not a forecastValley conditions vary · cave around 10°C and damp; thermal pool around 27°C
Cave conditions ↗ · Thermal pool ↗
Walking and paceCave steps + very steep pool walk
OvernightTumut · 2 of 2 nights

Tumut ↔ Yarrangobilly · about 2½–3 hr driving return; a full-day outing

  • Morning: Drive to Yarrangobilly and visit the cave experience confirmed as suitable for your baby and carrier. South Glory is self-guided, with 206 steps inside plus the approach track.
  • Midday: Picnic in the valley and allow an unhurried rest before deciding on the pool walk.
  • Afternoon: Visit the spring-fed thermal pool if the walk and conditions suit, then return to Tumut. A cave-only or pool-only outing is a valid scaled-down day.

The thermal pool is around 27°C, reached by a very steep 700m one-way walk, Grade 4. The main pool is 2.5m deep, with a separate wading pool. South Glory is about 10°C and damp, with a 500m approach and a 470m cave route. Confirm carrier clearance, infant arrangements and tour availability with the visitor centre before booking; a backpack carrier can be awkward in caves.

3. Other choices, without overfilling the day

These are replacements or optional extensions. The main plan already has room to enjoy each place.

Balloon Aloft Canberra · optional sunrise flight

First choice: 25 December sunrise, only if the operator confirms Christmas service. Allow 3–4 hours total for a 45–60-minute flight; breakfast adds about an hour. Meet at the Hyatt Hotel Canberra roughly 30 minutes before sunrise, at the time advised by the operator. Minimum age 6: your baby cannot fly, and needs a non-flying adult. If using 26 December as a confirmed backup, replace Questacon that morning and move Questacon to 24 December only with an early enough arrival, or to the return journey with a revised Canberra stop. Do not squeeze both into the departure morning. Check the date-specific fare; tickets and breakfast are extra to the trip budget. Weather cancellations can mean rebooking or a refund under the operator’s terms.

Zoo or Tidbinbilla

Both replace Questacon on 26 December. The zoo opens at 9:30am; allow about three hours. The Sanctuary at Tidbinbilla offers bushland and wetlands but adds access driving. Both close Christmas Day.

Christmas opening hours

Canberra Centre lists 24 Dec 9am–5pm, 25 Dec closed and 26 Dec 8am–6pm. Questacon, the zoo and Tidbinbilla close Christmas Day. Arboretum grounds remain the outdoor choice, with the café and visitor centre closed. Confirm individual restaurants and holiday reception hours.

Small walks, big weather

Summer alpine weather can change quickly. Bring warm, waterproof and sun-protective layers for both you and the baby. Short distance does not imply pram access: steps, wet rock, creek crossings and wind matter. Keep a turnaround option and choose dry, stable footing.
